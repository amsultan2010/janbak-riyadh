## What that means in short

This summary is not the licence. The linked text is.

You may copy, share, adapt and build on the dataset, for any purpose, as long as you:

- **Attribute.** Say that your work uses the Janbak dataset and OpenStreetMap, and keep
  the notices above. A line such as "Data: Janbak and OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0"
  with a link to this repository is enough.
- **Share alike.** If you publish an adapted version of the dataset, release it under the
  same licence.
- **Keep it open.** If you add technical restrictions to a copy, also offer one without them.

## Why not MIT like the code

Part of the dataset comes from OpenStreetMap, which is under the Open Database License, and
that licence requires databases derived from it to stay under it.

## What it does not cover

- The code, interface text and design of the site: see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- The names, logos and trademarks of the places and agencies listed.
- The official websites the entries link to.

Phone numbers, opening hours and addresses are facts. Facts themselves are not owned by
anyone; the licence covers the collection, its structure and the work of checking it.
